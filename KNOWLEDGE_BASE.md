## Platform Overview

PerformPro is an AI-powered performance management system designed for enterprise organizations. It helps employees, managers, and HR teams track performance, set goals, give feedback, and plan career development.

### Key Features
- **Goals & OKRs**: Set, track, and align objectives across the organization
- **Continuous Feedback**: Give and receive real-time feedback
- **Performance Reviews**: Structured review cycles with 360-degree feedback
- **AI Insights**: Predictive analytics for flight risk, team health, and talent
- **Career Development**: Personalized development plans and learning paths
- **Skills Endorsements**: Peer-to-peer skill validation

### Role-Based Access
- **Employees**: View personal performance data, goals, feedback, and development plans
- **Managers**: Access team analytics, health scores, and individual performance data
- **HR/Recruiters**: Organization-wide analytics, 9-box matrix, and talent insights

## OKRs and Goals

### What are OKRs?

**OKR** stands for **Objectives and Key Results**. It's a goal-setting framework that helps align individual, team, and company objectives.

**Objective**: What you want to achieve (qualitative, inspirational)  
**Key Results**: How you measure success (quantitative, measurable)

### Example OKR
```
Objective: Improve customer satisfaction
Key Results:
- Increase NPS score from 45 to 60
- Reduce average response time to under 2 hours
- Achieve 95% customer retention rate
```

### Creating Goals in PerformPro

1. Navigate to your dashboard
2. Click **"Create Goal"** button
3. Enter goal details:
   - **Title**: Clear, concise objective
   - **Description**: Detailed explanation
   - **Due Date**: Target completion date
   - **Priority**: High, Medium, or Low
4. Add **Key Results** to track progress
5. Set **Visibility**: Personal, Team, Department, or Company-wide
6. Click **"Save Goal"**

### Goal Best Practices
- Make goals **SMART**: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound
- Align personal goals with team and company objectives
- Update progress regularly (at least weekly)
- Review and adjust goals quarterly
- Link goals to performance reviews

### Tracking Progress
- View progress bars on your dashboard
- Update key results as you make progress
- Set goals to "On Track," "At Risk," or "Completed"
- Review goal analytics in the Goals tab

### Training Resources for OKRs
- **Internal Training**: Contact your HR team for OKR workshops
- **Recommended Reading**: "Measure What Matters" by John Doerr
- **Online Courses**: Search for "OKR fundamentals" on LinkedIn Learning or Coursera
- **Templates**: Access goal templates in the PerformPro resource center

## Performance Reviews

### Review Types

1. **Self-Review**: Assess your own performance against goals and competencies
2. **Manager Review**: Your manager evaluates your performance
3. **360-Degree Review**: Feedback from peers, direct reports, and managers
4. **Annual Review**: Comprehensive year-end performance evaluation

### Review Cycle

- **Q1**: Goal setting and planning
- **Q2**: Mid-year check-in
- **Q3**: Progress review and adjustment
- **Q4**: Annual performance review

### Completing a Review

1. Navigate to **Performance Reviews** section
2. Click on the pending review
3. Rate yourself on:
   - **Goal Achievement**: Progress on objectives
   - **Competencies**: Technical skills, leadership, communication, etc.
   - **Overall Performance**: 1-5 scale
4. Provide written comments with specific examples
5. Submit for manager review

### Review Scoring

- **5.0 - Exceptional**: Far exceeds expectations, top performer
- **4.0 - Exceeds Expectations**: Consistently delivers above target
- **3.0 - Meets Expectations**: Solid performance, meets all requirements
- **2.0 - Developing**: Improving but needs more growth
- **1.0 - Needs Improvement**: Below expectations, action plan required

### After Your Review
- Discuss results with your manager in a 1-on-1
- Create development plan based on feedback
- Set new goals for the next cycle
- Track progress on improvement areas

## Feedback System

### Types of Feedback

1. **Positive**: Recognize great work and strengths
2. **Constructive**: Suggest improvements with actionable advice
3. **Recognition**: Public acknowledgment of achievements
4. **Coaching**: Guide development and skill-building

### Giving Effective Feedback

**Use the SBI Model**:
- **Situation**: Describe the specific context
- **Behavior**: Explain what you observed
- **Impact**: Share the effect it had

**Example**:
```
Situation: "During yesterday's client presentation..."
Behavior: "...you clearly explained the technical architecture..."
Impact: "...which helped the client understand our solution and move forward with the contract."
```

### Requesting Feedback

1. Go to **Feedback** section
2. Click **"Request Feedback"**
3. Select colleague(s)
4. Specify:
   - What you want feedback on
   - Specific projects or skills
   - Timeline for response
5. Send request

### Receiving Feedback
- Review all feedback in your Feedback tab
- Acknowledge feedback to show you've read it
- Reflect on patterns and themes
- Create action items for improvement areas
- Thank colleagues who provide feedback

## AI Insights

PerformPro uses artificial intelligence to provide data-driven insights about performance, team health, and talent.

### Flight Risk Prediction

**What it is**: AI predicts the likelihood an employee might leave the organization

**Factors analyzed**:
- Performance review trends
- Feedback sentiment and frequency
- Goal completion rates
- Time since last promotion
- Engagement patterns
- Peer comparison

**Risk Levels**:
- **Low Risk** (0-30%): Employee is engaged and stable
- **Medium Risk** (31-60%): Some concerns, monitor closely
- **High Risk** (61-100%): Immediate retention action needed

**For Employees**: Use insights to discuss career growth with your manager  
**For Managers**: Proactively engage with at-risk team members

### Team Health Score

**What it is**: Overall team performance and engagement metric (0-100)

**Components**:
- Average performance ratings
- Goal completion rates
- Feedback culture strength
- Team engagement levels
- Collaboration metrics

**Score Interpretation**:
- **80-100**: Excellent - High-performing, engaged team
- **60-79**: Good - Solid team with some improvement areas
- **40-59**: Fair - Needs attention and intervention
- **0-39**: Poor - Immediate action required

### 9-Box Talent Matrix

**What it is**: Visual grid mapping performance vs. potential

**Matrix Positions**:
- **Box 9 - Stars**: High performance, high potential (succession planning)
- **Box 8 - High Potential**: Medium performance, high potential (fast-track development)
- **Box 7 - Rough Diamond**: Low performance, high potential (needs coaching)
- **Box 6 - Core Player**: High performance, medium potential (solid contributor)
- **Box 5 - Solid Performer**: Medium performance, medium potential (majority of workforce)
- **Box 4 - Inconsistent**: Low performance, medium potential (performance management)
- **Box 3 - Trusted Pro**: High performance, low potential (valued specialist)
- **Box 2 - Effective**: Medium performance, low potential (solid worker)
- **Box 1 - Needs Attention**: Low performance, low potential (requires action)

### AI Insight Types

**For Employees**:
- Technical Excellence (strengths)
- Leadership Potential (opportunities)
- Consistent High Performance (achievements)
- Cross-functional Collaboration (growth areas)
- Goal Progress Alerts (action items)
- Feedback Culture Champion (engagement)

**For Managers**:
- Strong Team Performance (team strengths)
- Development Focus Needed (team gaps)
- Flight Risk Detected (retention alerts)
- Goal Achievement Rate (team metrics)
- Feedback Participation (culture indicators)

## Skills and Endorsements

### What are Skills?

Skills are specific competencies tracked in PerformPro across 8 categories:
1. **Technical**: Programming, tools, technologies
2. **Leadership**: Team management, decision-making, strategic thinking
3. **Communication**: Writing, presenting, active listening
4. **Collaboration**: Teamwork, cross-functional work, relationship building
5. **Problem Solving**: Analytical thinking, creativity, troubleshooting
6. **Project Management**: Planning, execution, resource management
7. **Domain Expertise**: Industry knowledge, subject matter expertise
8. **Soft Skills**: Emotional intelligence, adaptability, time management

### Adding Skills

1. Go to your profile
2. Navigate to **Skills** section
3. Click **"Add Skill"**
4. Select category
5. Enter skill name
6. Set proficiency level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Expert)

### Endorsing Colleagues

**Why endorse**: Validate peers' skills based on direct observation

**How to endorse**:
1. Visit colleague's profile
2. View their skills list
3. Click **"Endorse"** on skills you've witnessed
4. Provide optional comment for context

**Endorsement Etiquette**:
- Only endorse skills you've directly observed
- Be genuine and specific
- Endorse diverse team members
- Return endorsements when appropriate

### Skill Development

- Identify skill gaps through reviews and feedback
- Add new skills as you learn them
- Update proficiency levels as you improve
- Seek endorsements from colleagues who've seen your work
- Use skills data to guide development plans

## Best Practices

### Goal Setting
- Align personal goals with team and company objectives
- Make goals SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound)
- Update progress weekly
- Review and adjust quarterly

### Feedback Culture
- Give feedback in real-time, not just during reviews
- Be specific with examples
- Balance positive and constructive feedback
- Follow up on feedback received

### Performance Reviews
- Prepare thoroughly with specific examples
- Be honest and reflective
- Focus on growth and development
- Create actionable plans from review outcomes

### Career Development
- Update your development plan quarterly
- Seek diverse learning opportunities
- Take on stretch assignments
- Build relationships across the organization

### AI Insights
- Review insights regularly
- Discuss concerning trends with your manager
- Use insights to guide development priorities
- Track progress on insight-based action items
